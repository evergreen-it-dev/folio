# Getting help

## Where to ask

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Something is broken | [Open a bug report](https://github.com/evergreen-it-dev/folio/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| An idea or a missing feature | [Open a feature request](https://github.com/evergreen-it-dev/folio/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| A vulnerability | Privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

Before opening an issue, check
[docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md#when-something-goes-wrong) and
[docs/LIMITATIONS.md](docs/LIMITATIONS.md) — the answer may already be there.

## What helps us help you

- the version or commit of Folio;
- how it is installed (Docker Compose, a platform, from source);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the relevant part of `docker compose logs app`.

Remove passwords, tokens and private content from anything you post.

## What to expect

Folio is maintained by a small team. Issues are read, but there is no
guaranteed response time, and not every feature request will be implemented.

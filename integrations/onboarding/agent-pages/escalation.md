# Escalation

When the pages cannot answer, or the question is about a person, a contract or money outside the published price list, send the newcomer to a human and say who.

| Topic | Ask |
|---|---|
| Their plan, goals, feedback | Their manager |
| Tools, accounts, access | Replace with the name of the person who owns IT access |
| Pricing exceptions, discounts | Replace with the name of the person who approves them |
| Anything about pay, contracts or HR | Replace with the HR contact |

Replace the placeholders with real names and keep this page up to date: the assistant sends people exactly where it says.
